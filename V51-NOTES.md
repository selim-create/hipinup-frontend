# Hipinup v5.1 refinement

This pass follows the visual review of the merged v5 homepage.

Focus areas:
- simplify and strengthen the masthead/header
- turn the hero into a real magazine-cover composition with typography inside the image
- reduce the boxed feel of the live pulse and secondary stories
- tighten Editors Desk into one continuous spread
- make fashion feel assembled rather than gridded
- replace the pink trend list with a blue/yellow/ink radar poster wall
- replace the repeated wellness card row with a collage composition
- compress topic navigation into a transition strip before the newsletter/footer

The existing v5 files remain intact so the refinement can be compared and rolled back easily.
